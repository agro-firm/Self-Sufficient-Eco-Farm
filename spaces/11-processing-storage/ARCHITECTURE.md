# Agro-Processing & Storage Hub — Architecture

## Architectural intent

One compact support hub with hard internal separation between dusty, clean-food, wet, cold, hot-work and dry-fire-load functions.

## L1 placement

X 31.680–84.467 m; Y 12.828–65.871 m. L1 block ≈52.787 × 53.043 m.

## Adjacency

- West access/service strip
- Energy/water east
- Vegetables north
- Perimeter service road south

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- crop receiving/drying
- grain storage
- rice milling
- feed milling
- oil pressing
- seed bank
- cold chain
- clean food handling
- workshop
- feed logistics

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **2,800 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
