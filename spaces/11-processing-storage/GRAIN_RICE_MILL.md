# Grain Store & Rice Mill

Parent: [Processing & Storage Hub](README.md)

Base planning:
- grain store: **400 m²**, base capacity **10–20 t dry grain**
- rice mill room: **200 m²**
- base mill throughput: **~100–200 kg paddy/hour**
- larger storage or up to ~300 kg/hour requires justified custom/external milling demand

Flow: receiving → moisture check/drying → grain store → rice mill → rice/bran/broken rice/husk separation.

Keep food grain, feed grain and seed grain identified/separated. Control dust, rodents, moisture and ignition sources.
