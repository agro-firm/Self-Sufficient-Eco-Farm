# Feed Mill, Silage & Hay

Parent: [Processing & Storage Hub](README.md)

Base planning:
- feed mill: **270 m²**
- silage/hay/feed logistics: **280 m²**
- ruminant roughage reserve: **60–90 days**
- poultry/fish formulated feed reserve: **14–30 days**

Feed mill: receive → clean → grind → micro-ingredient weigh → mix → optional pelletize → bag/store.

Silage must be sealed and drainage-controlled. Hay must be dry, ventilated and fire-separated from hot work.
