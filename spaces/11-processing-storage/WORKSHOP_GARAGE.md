# Workshop & Garage

Parent: [Processing & Storage Hub](README.md)

Base planning inside the hub:
- workshop/spares: **260 m²**
- vehicle/implement turning and loading also use the hub's shared circulation/service area

Functions: tractor/car/implement service, tools, spares, charging, lubricants, controlled hot work and repair.

Use secondary containment for oils/chemicals. Oily washwater routes through an oil-water separator/hazardous stream. Keep hay/grain dust and clean packhouse separated from welding/hot work.
