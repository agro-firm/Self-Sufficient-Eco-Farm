# Cold Chain & Packhouse

Parent: [Processing & Storage Hub](README.md)

Base planning:
- cold room + packhouse: **300 m²**
- milk/egg/fish clean handling: **170 m²**

Clean flow: receive → inspect/wash as appropriate → grade → pack → rapid chill/cold store → dispatch.

Keep dirty animal crates, manure equipment, workshop oils and dusty mill air out of the clean handling route. Cold chain remains a critical electrical load.
