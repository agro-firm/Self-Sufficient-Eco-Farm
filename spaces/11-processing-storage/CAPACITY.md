# Agro-Processing & Storage Hub — Capacity

## Parent space

- Area: **2,800 m²**
- Capacity status: master-planning / L1
- Capacity must remain inside the approved parent area and utility limits.

## Physical / operating capacity

- Grain store 400 m²; base 10–20 t dry grain capacity
- Rice mill 200 m²; ~100–200 kg paddy/hour base
- Feed mill 270 m²
- Cold room + packhouse 300 m²
- Clean milk/egg/fish handling 170 m²
- Workshop/spares 260 m²

## Expansion gate

Do not increase capacity unless all of the following remain within limits:
- land/space
- feed or raw-material availability
- water
- energy
- labor
- drainage/waste treatment
- biosecurity/safety
- storage/processing
- market demand where sale output is expected

## Capacity records

Track installed/design capacity, actual used capacity, peak use, spare capacity, downtime and the constraint that currently limits expansion.
