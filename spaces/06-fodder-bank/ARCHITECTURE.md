# Dedicated Fodder Bank — Architecture

## Architectural intent

Largest productive block by design. Direct north-south relationship to livestock shortens feed transport.

## L1 placement

X 83.336–237.172 m; Y 65.871–143.877 m. L1 block ≈153.836 × 78.005 m.

## Adjacency

- Vegetables west
- Ruminants/poultry/biogas south
- Food/orchard north

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- Napier
- fodder maize/sorghum
- legume forage
- seasonal fodder
- silage production
- animal feed security

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **12,000 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
