# Dedicated Fodder Bank — Detailed Description

## What this space does

- Napier
- fodder maize/sorghum
- legume forage
- seasonal fodder
- silage production
- animal feed security

## Why it is placed here

Largest productive block by design. Direct north-south relationship to livestock shortens feed transport.

## Main dependencies

- [`eco-farm-fodder-bank-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-fodder-bank-space/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-fodder-feed`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-fodder-feed/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-ruminant-livestock`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-ruminant-livestock/SKILL.md)

## Interfaces

### Required / useful neighbors
- Vegetables west
- Ruminants/poultry/biogas south
- Food/orchard north

### Controlled / prohibited interfaces
- waterlogging
- dirty wastewater
- decorative land conversion
- unmeasured herd expansion

## Utility dependencies

- irrigation
- field drainage
- harvest/cart lanes
- silage-haul connection

## Drainage / wastewater principle

Keep fodder well drained; use clean irrigation and controlled nutrient application.

## Main risks

- loss of allocated area to unrelated uses
- cross-contamination between clean and dirty systems
- flood/cyclone damage
- poor maintenance access
- unplanned utility crossings
- future expansion without feed/water/energy/waste capacity checks

## Verification checklist
