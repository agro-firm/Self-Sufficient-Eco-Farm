# Poultry & Duck District — Detailed Description

## What this space does

- layer house
- broiler house
- duck house
- duck wet pad
- egg handling
- bird isolation

## Why it is placed here

Three physically separated bird systems inside one parent district with controlled entry.

## Main dependencies

- [`eco-farm-poultry-duck-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-poultry-duck-space/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-poultry-duck`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-poultry-duck/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-biosecurity-animal-health`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-biosecurity-animal-health/SKILL.md)

## Interfaces

### Required / useful neighbors
- Quarantine west
- Ruminants east
- Fodder north
- Perimeter service road south

### Controlled / prohibited interfaces
- mixing species equipment
- direct canal discharge
- clean packhouse traffic
- central fish pond creation

## Utility dependencies

- drinking water
- ventilation/lighting
- feed points
- egg route
- wastewater treatment connection

## Drainage / wastewater principle

Duck/poultry washwater is screened/settled/treated; never direct to canal.

## Main risks

- loss of allocated area to unrelated uses
- cross-contamination between clean and dirty systems
- flood/cyclone damage
- poor maintenance access
- unplanned utility crossings
- future expansion without feed/water/energy/waste capacity checks
