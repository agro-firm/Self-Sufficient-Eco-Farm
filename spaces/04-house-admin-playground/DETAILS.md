# House, Admin, Playground & Kitchen Garden — Detailed Description

## What this space does

- residence
- farm administration
- CCTV/NVR control
- first aid/emergency shelter
- play/recreation
- household kitchen garden

## Why it is placed here

Compact functional farm residence/control center. Playground and kitchen garden are internal components, not separate land allocations.

- [`eco-farm-house-admin-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-house-admin-space/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-playground-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-playground-space/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-electrical-lighting-cctv`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-electrical-lighting-cctv/SKILL.md)

## Interfaces

### Required / useful neighbors
- West clean access/buffer strip
- Food-crop zone east
- Perimeter fire road north

### Controlled / prohibited interfaces
- biogas/compost
- workshop hot-work
- quarantine
- manure lanes
- heavy truck route

## Utility dependencies

- potable water
- blackwater/greywater
- critical power
- communications/fiber
- CCTV
- low-glare lighting

## Drainage / wastewater principle

Human blackwater and greywater route to appropriate treatment; roof rainwater goes to clean stormwater harvesting.

## Main risks

- loss of allocated area to unrelated uses
- cross-contamination between clean and dirty systems
- flood/cyclone damage
- poor maintenance access
- unplanned utility crossings
- future expansion without feed/water/energy/waste capacity checks
