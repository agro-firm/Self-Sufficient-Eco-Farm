# House, Admin, Playground & Kitchen Garden — Cost Model

## CAPEX categories

- house/admin structure
- playground surface/shade
- parking/path
- office/CCTV/NVR
- potable water/sanitation

Do not treat these categories as quoted prices. Final CAPEX requires site quantities, specification, supplier quotations, installation, testing, contingency and commissioning.

## Annual OPEX planning envelope

- Lower planning envelope: **BDT 100,000/year**
- Base planning envelope: **BDT 175,000/year**
- High planning envelope: **BDT 250,000/year**

These are **budget placeholders**, not market quotations. Replace them with local labor, input, maintenance and utility prices before investment approval.

## Cost ledger

Separate:
1. purchased materials/inputs
2. hired labor
3. family/owner labor (optional imputed economic cost)
4. energy/fuel
5. veterinary/testing/compliance
6. packaging/transport
7. maintenance/spares
8. replacement reserve
9. losses/mortality/spoilage
10. finance/depreciation if doing full accounting

## Decision rule

Never expand because gross revenue rises if marginal feed, labor, energy, disease, wastewater, storage or replacement cost rises faster.
