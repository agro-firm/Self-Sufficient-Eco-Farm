# House, Admin, Playground, Garage & Swimming Pool — Design Rules

## Design intent
A comfortable but practical farm home/control center with a 2-story duplex house, garage, playground and secured pool.

## Non-negotiable
- stay inside 2,200 m²
- no vegetable garden
- no heavy farm workshop
- no manure/service truck route
- pool and playground separated by a controlled barrier
- crop-side edge kept low/open to minimize shade
- solar prioritized on house/garage roofs
- all hardscape drains to controlled systems

## Materials
Use durable, moisture/corrosion-resistant, repairable materials suitable for Bangladesh heat, monsoon and cyclone exposure.

## Landscape
Only functional shade, privacy, drainage/rain-garden and safety planting. Do not create large ornamental lawn areas.
