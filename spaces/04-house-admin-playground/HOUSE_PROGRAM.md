Residence + admin/security functions as described in ARCHITECTURE.md.

## First floor
Family/private sleeping/lounge functions.

## Roof
- solar PV priority
- lightning protection
- controlled maintenance access
- no mandatory rooftop garden
- roof plant only where structurally/maintenance appropriate

## Accessibility / safety
Provide at least one accessible ground-level route, safe stairs/guardrails, smoke/heat alarms, emergency lighting and clear exits.
