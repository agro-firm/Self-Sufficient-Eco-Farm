# House, Admin, Playground, Garage & Swimming Pool — Architecture

## Architectural direction
Use a **two-story modern tropical duplex/farm-bungalow hybrid**, large enough for family living and admin/control functions but still compact relative to the farm.

## Building envelope
- footprint target: ~24 × 20 m = 480 m²
- floors: 2
- gross floor-area planning range: ~800–960 m² depending voids/verandas/terraces
- exact column grid, stairs, walls and room sizes: L2/L3

## Ground floor planning
- covered entrance/foyer
- clean visitor reception
- farm office/admin
- CCTV/NVR/security/control room
- first-aid/emergency room
- family living
- dining
- kitchen/pantry
- guest/flexible bedroom
- common toilet
- utility/laundry
- vertical circulation
- shaded verandas where useful

## First floor planning
- master suite
- 2–3 family bedrooms/flexible rooms
- family lounge/study
- bathrooms/wardrobes
- balconies/shaded external spaces
- storage
- emergency egress strategy

## Climate
- cross-ventilation
- deep overhangs/shading
- protected openings
- cyclone-resilient roof/load path
- roof drainage sized by engineering
- daylight without excessive solar heat gain

## Site relationship
Garage and arrival remain on clean access side; pool is physically separated from playground; playground remains low-height next to the food-crop edge.
