# House, Admin, Playground & Kitchen Garden — Architecture

## Architectural intent

Compact functional farm residence/control center. Playground and kitchen garden are internal components, not separate land allocations.

## L1 placement

X 28.627–63.384 m; Y 143.877–207.172 m. L1 block ≈34.758 × 63.296 m.

## Adjacency

- West clean access/buffer strip
- Food-crop zone east
- Perimeter fire road north

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- residence
- farm administration
- CCTV/NVR control
- first aid/emergency shelter
- play/recreation
- household kitchen garden

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **2,200 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
