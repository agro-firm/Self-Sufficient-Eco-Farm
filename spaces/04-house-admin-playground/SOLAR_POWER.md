# House/Garage Solar Power

## Concept
Reserve roof surfaces on the house and garage for PV. A **10–25 kWp local roof allocation** is a feasibility concept only; final installed capacity must be coordinated with the farm-wide 120–160 kWp system, roof structure, shade and load study.

## Electrical relationship
PV feeds the farm microgrid/house sub-main as engineered. Central battery authority remains the energy/water-control system unless an approved local backup module is added.

## Loads to favor in daylight
- pool filtration
- water heating/pumping where appropriate
- EV charging
- laundry and discretionary house loads

## Safety
PV uplift, isolation, surge protection, earthing/lightning, firefighter access and roof maintenance require engineering.
