# Residential/Admin Garage

## Allocation
120 m² concept, approximately 12 × 10 m.

## Use
- 2–3 cars/SUVs depending final layout
- motorcycles/bicycles
- optional EV charger
- light cleaning and storage

## Not allowed
- tractor/implement workshop
- welding/hot work
- bulk fuel/chemical storage

## Systems
Trench drain or controlled floor drainage, lighting, CCTV, fire extinguisher, EV circuit if used, roof PV where practical.
