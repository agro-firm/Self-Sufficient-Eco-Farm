# Swimming Pool

## Allocation
- water surface: ~60 m², concept ~12 × 5 m
- deck/safety/equipment allocation: 140 m²

## Function
Family recreation only.

## Safety
- child-resistant fence/gate or equivalent controlled barrier
- non-slip deck
- depth markings
- rescue ring/pole
- supervised access
- CCTV on approaches/pool perimeter, not private indoor areas
- compliant pool bonding and residual-current protection
- separate equipment enclosure

## Water/drainage
Pool fill/makeup is metered. Backwash and chlorinated drain-down route to an approved controlled treatment/disposal path; never directly to perimeter canal or crop drain.

## Image rule
Pool must remain a modest rectangular residential pool, not a resort lagoon.
