# Vegetables, Greenhouse & Nursery — Detailed Description

## What this space does

- open vegetable beds
- greenhouse/protected crops
- nursery/mother plants
- clean wash/service point

## Why it is placed here

Open beds dominate, with compact greenhouse and nursery structures arranged for hygiene and service access.

## Main dependencies

- [`eco-farm-vegetable-greenhouse-nursery-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-vegetable-greenhouse-nursery-space/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-crops-horticulture`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-crops-horticulture/SKILL.md)
- [`eco-farm-water-treatment-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-water-treatment-space/SKILL.md)

## Interfaces

### Required / useful neighbors
- West clean access strip
- Fodder east
- House/crops north
- Processing south-west

### Controlled / prohibited interfaces
- animal manure traffic
- raw slurry irrigation
- dirty crates
- uncontrolled shade

## Utility dependencies

- clean irrigation
- drip lines where useful
- nursery water
- clean wash point
- drains

## Drainage / wastewater principle

Clean field runoff to swales; washwater to appropriate clean/processing treatment route.

## Main risks

- loss of allocated area to unrelated uses
- cross-contamination between clean and dirty systems
- flood/cyclone damage
- poor maintenance access
- unplanned utility crossings
- future expansion without feed/water/energy/waste capacity checks
