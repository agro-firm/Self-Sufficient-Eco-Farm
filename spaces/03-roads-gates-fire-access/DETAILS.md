# Perimeter Roads, Gates & Fire Access — Detailed Description

## What this space does

- fire access
- service circulation
- gate approaches
- maintenance
- edge drainage

## Why it is placed here

This 4,200 m² allocation belongs primarily to the perimeter road ring. Internal access spines are funded from the separate 3,000 m² internal-access/buffer allocation.

- [`eco-farm-roads-gates-traffic-space`](../../.agents/skills/eco-farm-roads-gates-traffic-space/SKILL.md)

## Interfaces

### Required / useful neighbors
- Canal outside
- Internal farm core inside
- Internal access/buffer strips connect from west side

### Controlled / prohibited interfaces
- playground through-traffic
- manure traffic on clean packhouse route
- dead-end emergency access
- unverified turning geometry

## Utility dependencies

- road lighting
- CCTV
- utility crossing sleeves
- edge drains
- signage/gate controls

## Drainage / wastewater principle

Road runoff goes to suitable swales/silt control, not dirty-treatment lines.

## Main risks

- loss of allocated area to unrelated uses
- cross-contamination between clean and dirty systems
- flood/cyclone damage
- poor maintenance access
- unplanned utility crossings
- future expansion without feed/water/energy/waste capacity checks
