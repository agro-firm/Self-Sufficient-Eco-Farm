# Perimeter Roads, Gates & Fire Access — Architecture

## Architectural intent

This 4,200 m² allocation belongs primarily to the perimeter road ring. Internal access spines are funded from the separate 3,000 m² internal-access/buffer allocation.

## L1 placement

Road ring lies between canal inner control at inset 7.927 m and internal-core control at inset 12.828 m; average width ≈ 4.901 m. Main gate X 15–21 m south edge; service gate X 222–227 m south edge.

## Adjacency

- Canal outside
- Internal farm core inside
- Internal access/buffer strips connect from west side

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- fire access
- service circulation
- gate approaches
- maintenance
- edge drainage

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **4,200 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
