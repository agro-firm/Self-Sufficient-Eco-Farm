# Human Food Crop Zone — Architecture

## Architectural intent

Maintain a large uninterrupted solar envelope. Seasonal subdivisions may change without moving the outer L1 block.

## L1 placement

X 63.384–166.077 m; Y 143.877–207.172 m. L1 block ≈102.693 × 63.296 m.

## Adjacency

- House/admin west
- Orchard east
- Perimeter fire road north
- Fodder/vegetable production south

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- rice
- maize
- pulses/legumes
- mustard/sesame
- human food and feed by-products

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **6,500 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
