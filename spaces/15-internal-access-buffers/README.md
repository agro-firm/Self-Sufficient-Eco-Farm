## Main capacity/output snapshot

- Three 1,000 m² distributed west-side access/buffer strips
- Supports 5 m clean internal spine concept
- Provides headlands, swales, utility corridors, turning space and fire/biosecurity separation
- No sale output
- Service output: internal transport, drainage, maintenance, biosecurity separation, machinery turning and utility routing

## Required rule

Do not judge this space only by cash income. Some zones create direct sales, some create internal replacement value, and some protect the rest of the farm by reducing risk or operating cost.

See [farm-wide economic assumptions](../../docs/ECONOMIC_ASSUMPTIONS.md).
