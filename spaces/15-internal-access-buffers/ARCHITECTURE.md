# Internal Access Spines, Biosecurity Buffers, Headlands & Swales — Architecture

## Architectural intent

This is intentionally distributed rather than one rectangle. It makes the internal geometry operationally usable without stealing uncounted land.

## L1 placement

South strip X 12.828–31.680, Y 12.828–65.871; Middle strip X 12.828–25.648, Y 65.871–143.877; North strip X 12.828–28.627, Y 143.877–207.172. West clean spine concept X 16–21 m across internal core.

## Adjacency

- Touches all three internal horizontal bands
- Connects to main clean gate
- Interfaces with zone headlands

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- internal access
- biosecurity separation
- headlands/turning
- vegetated swales
- fire breaks
- utility corridors

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **3,000 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
