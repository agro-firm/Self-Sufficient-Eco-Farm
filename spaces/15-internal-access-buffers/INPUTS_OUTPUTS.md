# Internal Access Spines, Biosecurity Buffers, Headlands & Swales — Inputs & Outputs

## Main inputs

- Vegetation/swale maintenance
- Road/path maintenance
- Drain clearing
- Utility inspections
- Marking/signage

## Main outputs

- Reduced internal transport conflict
- Reduced waterlogging/erosion
- Fire/biosecurity separation
- Protected utility routes

## Circular-economy rule

Before purchasing an input, check whether another farm zone already produces a safe equivalent. Before discarding an output, determine whether it can safely become food, feed, bedding, compost, biogas feedstock, irrigation water, energy or another farm input.

## Prohibited shortcut

Self-sufficiency must never replace certified vaccines/medicine, required mineral nutrients, safe water treatment, specialist engineering or quality-critical inputs with unsafe homemade substitutes.

## Monthly material-balance record

Track opening stock + purchased input + internally transferred input - consumed - sold - lost = closing stock.
