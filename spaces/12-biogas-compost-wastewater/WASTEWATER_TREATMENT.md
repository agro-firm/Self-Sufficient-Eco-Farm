# Wastewater Treatment

Parent: [Biogas, Compost & Wastewater](README.md)

Separate incoming streams:
- human blackwater
- greywater
- livestock wastewater
- poultry/duck wastewater
- processing wastewater
- workshop oily water (separate pretreatment/hazardous route)

Concept logic:
- blackwater → septic/ABR/STP → secondary treatment/wetland → approved reuse/disposal
- greywater → screening/bio-treatment → suitable reuse
- animal wastewater → dry scrape/solid separation → digestion/equalization → treatment → controlled reuse
- processing wastewater → source-specific solids/grease removal → treatment

Clean stormwater never enters the sewage plant. No untreated wastewater enters the fish canal.
