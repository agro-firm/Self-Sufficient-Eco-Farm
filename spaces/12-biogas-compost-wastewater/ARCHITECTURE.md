# Biogas, Compost & Wastewater Treatment — Architecture

## Architectural intent

Dirty-resource recovery endpoint on far service side, immediately beside the main manure source.

## L1 placement

X 207.008–237.172 m; Y 12.828–65.871 m. L1 block ≈30.164 × 53.043 m.

## Adjacency

- Ruminants west
- Fodder north
- Perimeter service road south/east

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- manure receiving
- solid separation
- digester
- gas handling
- digestate
- composting
- wastewater treatment

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **1,600 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
