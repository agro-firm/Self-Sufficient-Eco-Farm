# Cattle, Goat & Sheep District — Economics

## Economic role

**Type:** productive

Gross example includes milk plus modeled goat-kid/lamb sales only. It excludes manure/biogas value and cattle capital appreciation. Purchased-feed share can change OPEX dramatically.

## Example scenario model

| Scenario | Example annual gross value | Example annual OPEX envelope | Gross value minus OPEX* |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | BDT 511,200 | BDT 1,100,000 | BDT -588,800 |
| Base | BDT 956,400 | BDT 700,000 | BDT 256,400 |
| Upside | BDT 1,529,600 | BDT 500,000 | BDT 1,029,600 |

\*This is **not net profit**. It excludes depreciation, finance cost, owner/family labor unless explicitly budgeted, central admin/security/road/energy overhead, taxes/fees, catastrophic loss, and CAPEX recovery.

## Required formulas

- Cash revenue = quantity sold × realized farm-gate/service price
- Internal replacement value = quantity used internally × price that would otherwise be paid
- Gross economic value = cash revenue + internal replacement value + measured avoided-cost value
- Cash OPEX = actual purchased inputs + hired labor + paid utilities/fuel + paid maintenance/services
- Operating contribution = gross economic value - cash OPEX
- Net profit requires depreciation, financing, taxes/fees, central overhead and replacement reserves

## Pricing rule

Use current **local farm-gate or realized selling prices**, not retail-market prices, unless the farm itself sells retail. Update prices monthly for volatile products.
