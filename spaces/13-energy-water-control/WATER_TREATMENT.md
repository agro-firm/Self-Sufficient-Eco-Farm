# Water Treatment

Parent: [Energy & Clean-Water Control](README.md)

Water classes:
1. human potable
2. food-processing clean water
3. animal drinking
4. irrigation
5. service/wash water
6. fire water

Treatment must match the source and use. Test bacteria, arsenic (if groundwater), iron/manganese where relevant, pH, EC/salinity, turbidity and other site-specific contaminants.

Keep wet treatment equipment physically separated from live electrical/battery rooms.
