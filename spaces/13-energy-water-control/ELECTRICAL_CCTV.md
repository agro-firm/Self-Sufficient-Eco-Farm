# Electrical, Lighting & CCTV

Parent: [Energy & Clean-Water Control](README.md)

Concept energy:
- rooftop solar: **120–160 kWp feasibility range**
- usable LFP battery: **300–500 kWh feasibility range**
- biogas generator: **10–20 kW initial planning envelope**, larger only if measured gas supply supports it

Security concept:
- road/security LED lighting with final spacing by photometric study
- fixed + PTZ CCTV at gates, corners, crossings and critical buildings
- PoE/fiber backbone where practical
- critical security/network equipment on backed power

Final cables, breakers, earthing, lightning, lens/FOV and protection settings require engineering.
