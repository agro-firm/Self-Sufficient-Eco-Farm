# Perimeter Canal — Business & Value Strategy

## How this space creates value

- Sell table fish in staggered batches
- Use nursery/hapa sections to reduce purchased fingerling handling risk
- Value irrigation/fire/flood services as avoided external cost rather than pretending they are cash sales
- Do not intensify fish stocking if it reduces flood reserve or water quality

## Before selling externally

1. Protect minimum household/farm internal requirements.
2. Protect emergency reserve.
3. Confirm storage/quality/traceability.
4. Compare internal replacement value with sale price.
5. Sell only the true surplus unless the space is explicitly a commercial production unit.

## Business KPIs

- cash revenue
- internal replacement value
- cash OPEX
- unit cost
- loss/spoilage/mortality
- labor hours
- capacity utilization
- working-capital days
- customer/payment risk where applicable

## Expansion rule

Expansion requires proof that incremental gross value exceeds incremental feed/input, labor, energy, maintenance, disease, waste-treatment, storage, finance and market-risk cost.
