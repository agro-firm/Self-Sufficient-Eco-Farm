# Perimeter Canal — Architecture

## Architectural intent

Treat the canal as a hydraulic/security ring first and an aquaculture system second. Maintain continuity, inspection access, fish escape control and overflow capacity.

## L1 placement

Outer control: inset 1.931 m. Inner canal control: inset 7.927 m. Derived planning water width ≈ 5.996 m.

## Adjacency

- Security wall/inspection band outside
- Perimeter fire/service road inside
- Gate/bridge crossings at approved access points

## Spaces / functions inside this zone

- fish production
- flood/stormwater retention
- irrigation reserve
- fire-water reserve
- security separation

## Architecture rules

- Keep the parent area at **5,400 m²**.
- Preserve clean/dirty traffic separation appropriate to this zone.
- Reserve maintenance and emergency access before adding decorative elements.
- Building footprints, internal partitions and exact equipment positions are **PROVISIONAL** until L2/L3 design.
- Any structure must respect flood, cyclone, fire, biosecurity and drainage requirements.
