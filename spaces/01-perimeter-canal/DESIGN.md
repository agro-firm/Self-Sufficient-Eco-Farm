# Perimeter Canal — Design Rules

## Design intent

Use stable engineered banks, erosion protection, screened intakes and controlled crossings. Final bank slope, lining, freeboard and culvert sizes require engineering.

## Core rules

- Preserve the L1 parent zone and area.
- Give function, safety, access, drainage and maintenance priority over appearance.
- Use durable, repairable, climate-appropriate materials.
- Avoid unnecessary lawns, fountains, decorative water bodies or structures that reduce productive/safety space.
- Design for Bangladesh heat, heavy rain, flood risk, high wind/cyclone exposure and corrosion/moisture.
- Treat all exact structural sizes as engineering work, not image-generation output.

## Environmental rules

- Keep clean water and dirty wastewater separated.
- Protect neighboring crop sunlight and airflow.
- Control dust, odor, noise and vectors at source.
- Retain permeable/vegetated surfaces where operationally compatible.
- Route organic by-products into approved feed, compost or biogas loops.

## Safety rules

- raw human sewage
- raw manure or poultry wastewater
- oily workshop water
- untreated processing wastewater
