# Perimeter Canal — Production & Service Output

## What this space can produce or deliver

- Fish: 1.2–2.0 t/year
- Monthly equivalent fish harvest: about 100–165 kg/month if staggered
- Service outputs: stored irrigation water, stormwater buffering, fire-water availability, security separation

## Output classification

- **Cash output:** product/service sold to an external customer.
- **Internal output:** product used by another farm zone instead of being purchased.
- **Protection/service output:** avoided loss, risk reduction, storage, access, treatment, water or energy continuity.

## Monthly / quarterly / annual planning

Where biological output is seasonal, do **not** divide annual output evenly and assume it occurs every month. Use actual crop/animal calendar, batch timing, lactation, harvest windows and storage.

## Records

Record quantity, unit, grade/quality, internal transfer, sale quantity, sale price, loss/waste, storage movement and destination.
