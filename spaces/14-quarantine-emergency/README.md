## Main capacity/output snapshot

- Independent controlled-entry animal isolation area
- Space for separate pens, examination and emergency supplies
- Capacity must follow veterinary disease-separation design, not maximum stocking
- No normal sale output
- Service output: disease containment, safe new-animal intake, emergency stock reserve

## Required rule

Do not judge this space only by cash income. Some zones create direct sales, some create internal replacement value, and some protect the rest of the farm by reducing risk or operating cost.

See [farm-wide economic assumptions](../../docs/ECONOMIC_ASSUMPTIONS.md).
