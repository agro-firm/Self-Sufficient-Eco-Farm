# Biosecurity and Animal Health

## Workflow
1. New animals enter quarantine.
2. Keep isolation for sick animals.
3. Enforce clean-to-dirty worker movement.
4. Use dedicated tools, footwear, crates, and equipment by zone.
5. Control visitors, rodents, vectors, feed contamination, and dead-stock handling.
6. Maintain vaccination, medicine, withdrawal, mortality, and disease records.
7. Define outbreak lockdown, disposal, cleaning, and restart procedures.

## Guardrails
Never invent vaccines, antibiotics, hormones, injectable medicines, or unverified treatments.
