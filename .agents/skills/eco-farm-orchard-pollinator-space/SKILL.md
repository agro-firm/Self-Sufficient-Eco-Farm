# Orchard and Pollinator Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**4,500 m²**.

Concept split:
- mango ~1,500 m²
- guava ~750 m²
- citrus ~750 m²
- banana/papaya ~1,000 m²
- bees/paths/drains/flower strips ~500 m²

## Rules

- place tallest mature trees where they do not shade the food-crop solar envelope
- calculate using mature canopy, not sapling size
- keep beehives away from playground/main family route
- provide service paths, mulch basins, irrigation, and drainage
- cyclone pruning/fall-risk management required
- do not plant dangerous tall trees close to PV roofs or critical buildings

## Blueprint rules

Show species zones, tree grid/spacing status, mature-canopy exclusion lines, beehive zone, paths, and irrigation/drainage.
