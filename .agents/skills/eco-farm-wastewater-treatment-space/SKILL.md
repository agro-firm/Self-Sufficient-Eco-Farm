# Wastewater Treatment Space

## Precision authority

Use the 1,600 m² biogas/compost/wastewater allocation together with `eco-farm-water-drainage-wastewater`. Exact unit sizes remain provisional until flow/loading calculations are complete.

## Separate incoming streams

- human blackwater
- human greywater
- livestock liquid/manure wash
- poultry/duck wastewater
- food-processing wastewater

Workshop oily water remains a separate pretreatment/hazardous stream and must not be blindly combined.

## Concept treatment logic

Human blackwater: septic/ABR or approved STP → secondary treatment/constructed wetland → compliant reuse/disposal.

Greywater: screening/appropriate pretreatment → biological/constructed-wetland treatment → suitable reuse.

Animal wastewater: dry scraping/solid separation → equalization/digestion where suitable → secondary treatment → controlled reuse.

Processing wastewater: source-specific screen/grease/solids removal → equalization/biological treatment as required.

## Rules

- clean stormwater does not enter sewage treatment
- no untreated discharge to fish canal
- sludge handling route must be explicit
- treatment must remain accessible for cleaning and desludging
- overflow under flood conditions must be engineered
- edible-crop direct-contact reuse is not assumed without approval
