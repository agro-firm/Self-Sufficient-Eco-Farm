# Electrical Distribution, Lighting and CCTV

## Authorities

Read `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md`, `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`, `eco-farm-energy-electrical`, and `eco-farm-security-resilience`.

## System scope

- main distribution board and sub-distribution
- underground power ducts
- critical/noncritical circuits
- solar/battery/generator interconnection
- road/security lights
- gate lights
- playground low-glare lights
- CCTV fixed/PTZ cameras
- PoE/fiber network
- NVR/control room
- UPS/battery backup
- earthing, surge and lightning interfaces

## Planning rules

- route utilities primarily along road/service corridors
- do not cut diagonally across crop blocks unless unavoidable
- do not place live electrical cabinets in flood-prone low areas
- separate power and communications as required by engineering/code
- provide access for cable pulling, inspection and repair
- critical CCTV/network/security circuits stay on backed power

## Lighting concept

Perimeter LED lights: approximately 38–47 at ~20–25 m concept spacing before photometric study.
Internal road/building lights: approximately 15–25.
Total road/security lighting concept: approximately 55–70.

Lighting must avoid excessive glare into house, animals, neighboring land, and pollinator areas.

## CCTV concept

- fixed cameras: approximately 20–26
- PTZ/high-coverage cameras: approximately 4–6
- total concept: approximately 24–32
- mandatory coverage: gates, corners, canal crossings, processing, stores, animal zones, biogas, energy/water control, playground approaches

## Blueprint output

Show separate symbols/line types for MV/LV if applicable, LV power, critical power, lighting, CCTV/PoE, fiber, earthing, generator, battery, solar, and control. Include legend and equipment IDs.

## Stop conditions

Exact poles, lux levels, cable sizes, voltage drop, breakers, CCTV lens/FOV, network bandwidth, earthing, lightning protection and trench separation require engineering and site survey.
