# Procurement and Self-Sufficiency

## Internal first
Fodder, roughage, silage, hay, many crops, seedlings, many OP seeds, compost, digestate, biogas, routine energy, irrigation water, rice milling, feed mixing, storage, basic repair.

## External when needed
Vaccines, medicines, mineral/vitamin premixes, precision feed inputs, selected certified/hybrid seed, genetics/semen, deficient nutrients, technology replacements, filters, lubricants, PPE, test reagents, critical spares.

## Workflow
Classify purchase necessity, calculate annual use/reserve, identify safe internal substitutes, compare lifecycle reliability/cost, and avoid false self-sufficiency that creates health or productivity risk.
