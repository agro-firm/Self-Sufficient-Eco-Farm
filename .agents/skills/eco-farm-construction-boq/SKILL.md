# Construction and BOQ

## Workflow
1. Confirm surveys, geotechnical data, flood level, utility loads, hydraulic calculations, and approved drawings.
2. Break work into civil, structural, drainage, electrical, solar, biogas, farm buildings, processing, security, and landscape packages.
3. Separate concept quantities from measured/tender quantities.
4. Define specification, unit, quantity, acceptance test, warranty, dependency, and commissioning.
5. Sequence earthworks/water systems before high-value equipment.
6. Require as-built drawings and commissioning records.

## Guardrails
Never create authoritative BOQ quantities from concept images alone.
