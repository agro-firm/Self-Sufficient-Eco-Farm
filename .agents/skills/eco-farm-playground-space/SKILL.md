# Playground Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Fixed planning size

**20,000 mm × 30,000 mm = 600 m²**.

## Uses

- children play
- badminton
- mini football
- exercise
- emergency assembly

## Safety rules

Must be:
- inside/next to house-admin clean zone
- away from canal edge
- away from biogas/compost
- away from workshop and truck paths
- away from quarantine and chemical storage
- visible to CCTV
- reachable by pedestrian route without crossing dirty traffic

## Design components

- low-maintenance permeable/grass surface
- shade structure
- drinking-water point
- low fence where needed
- seating
- low-glare lighting
- emergency assembly sign/location

## Image rules

Do not convert the playground into a decorative lawn, pool, or large landscaped park.
