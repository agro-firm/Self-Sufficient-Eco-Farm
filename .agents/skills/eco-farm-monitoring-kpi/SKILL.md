# Monitoring and KPIs

## Track
Crop yield/irrigation/soil; fodder dry matter/reserves; milk/eggs/births/mortality; fish water quality/biomass; solar/battery/biogas; rainfall/canal/water/wastewater; grain moisture/cold-room/feed stock; purchased vs internal inputs.

## Core KPIs
Feed self-sufficiency, energy self-sufficiency, water self-sufficiency, nutrient recycling, mortality, post-harvest loss, purchased-input share, emergency-reserve days.

## Workflow
Define owner, frequency, unit, source, acceptable range, alert threshold, corrective action, and monthly/quarterly review for each KPI.
