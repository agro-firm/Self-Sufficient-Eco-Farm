# Energy and Water Control Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**800 m²**.

Concept split:
- battery/inverter ~150 m²
- water treatment ~150 m²
- pump/control ~100 m²
- storage/utility ~100 m²
- service/fire/buffer = balance

## Requirements

- above design flood level
- restricted access
- clean/ventilated environment
- separate wet water-treatment areas from live electrical areas
- emergency isolation
- fire detection/suppression strategy appropriate to selected battery chemistry
- clear service access
- communications/monitoring
- utility corridors radiating to farm systems
