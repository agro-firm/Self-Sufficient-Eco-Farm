# Cold Chain and Packhouse Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Clean flow

field/animal product → clean receiving → wash/inspection → grading → packing → rapid chilling/cold storage → dispatch.

## Required separation

- vegetable/fruit handling
- milk handling
- egg grading
- fish wet/chill handling
- medicine refrigerator as separate locked control if present

## Utilities

- potable/food-grade water where required
- washable surfaces
- trapped drains to proper treatment
- critical electrical bus
- temperature logging
- emergency backup
- hygienic handwash/change point

## Rule

Dirty animal crates/manure equipment must never enter the clean packhouse route.
