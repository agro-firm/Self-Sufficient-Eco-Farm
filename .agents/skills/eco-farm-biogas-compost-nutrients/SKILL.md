# Biogas, Compost and Nutrients

## Workflow
1. Quantify daily manure and suitable organic inputs.
2. Separate solids and exclude contaminated material.
3. Size digestion from total solids, dilution, retention time, temperature, and gas demand.
4. Route gas to cooking/heat and dispatchable generator use.
5. Treat/mature digestate before application.
6. Compost dry carbon material with suitable nitrogen-rich material.
7. Build annual N/P/K/S/micronutrient budgets including exported nutrients.
8. Purchase deficient minerals when recycling cannot close the balance.

## Guardrails
No untreated slurry to the canal. Never assume compost/digestate replaces all fertilizer.
