# Poultry and Duck Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**1,500 m²**.

Concept footprints:
- layer house 10×18 m = 180 m²
- broiler house 10×12 m = 120 m²
- duck house 10×12 m = 120 m²
- wet/service yards ~300–400 m²
- feed/egg/isolation/buffers = balance

Base capacity:
- layers 120–150
- broilers 80–120/batch
- ducks 60–80

## Rules

- physical separation between layer, broiler, duck
- no shared dirty equipment without sanitation
- duck wet area is managed, not a fish pond
- wastewater goes to treatment
- egg collection uses clean route
- biosecurity entry/boot/tool control required
