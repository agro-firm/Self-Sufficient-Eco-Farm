# Human Food Crop Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**6,500 m²**.

Planning split:
- rice ~3,000 m²
- maize ~1,500 m²
- pulses ~1,000 m²
- mustard/sesame ~1,000 m²
Seasonal rotation may change these sub-areas.

## Absolute rules

- no tall trees/buildings inside the crop solar envelope
- machinery headlands must remain usable
- irrigation access and field drains must be continuous
- dirty wastewater is prohibited
- field runoff enters the canal only after silt/erosion control

## By-products

Rice: grain, bran, straw, husk.
Maize: grain, stover.
Pulses: grain, haulm.
Oilseed: edible oil, oil cake.

Map every by-product to food, feed, bedding, compost, biogas, or approved biomass use.

## Image/blueprint rules

Show large uninterrupted open crop blocks; do not add decorative trees between rows unless specifically an approved low-impact windbreak.
