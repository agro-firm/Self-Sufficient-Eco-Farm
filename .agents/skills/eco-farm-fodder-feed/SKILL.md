# Fodder and Feed

## Core rule
Animal numbers follow feed dry-matter capacity.

## Workflow
1. Measure/estimate dry-matter yield by fodder crop and season.
2. Plan Napier, fodder maize/sorghum, legumes, crop residues, hay, and silage.
3. Maintain 60–90 days of ruminant roughage reserve.
4. Separate human-food grain, seed grain, and feed grain.
5. Use rice bran, broken rice, stover, pulse haulm, and oil cake only after safe ration formulation.
6. Calculate feed self-sufficiency and imported feed share.
7. Re-run herd capacity before expansion.

## Guardrails
Never use moldy feed. Poultry, fish, and ruminant rations are not interchangeable.
