# Perimeter Canal Aquaculture

## Authority
There is no separate base-plan fish pond.

## Workflow
1. Preserve flood-storage/hydraulic function before maximizing fish density.
2. Divide canal operation with screens, sluices, hapas, or sections.
3. Reserve screened irrigation intakes and fire-water suction points.
4. Design overflow to prevent fish escape and flood backflow.
5. Monitor DO, temperature, pH, ammonia, salinity/EC, turbidity, mortality, and biomass.
6. Keep sewage, manure wastewater, oily water, and untreated processing water out.
7. Use conservative stocking until real water quality proves capacity.
