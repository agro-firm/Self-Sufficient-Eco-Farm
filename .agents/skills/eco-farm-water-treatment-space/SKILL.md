# Water Treatment Space

## Precision authority

Part of the 800 m² energy/water-control allocation. Exact equipment pads and tank sizes remain provisional until water demand and water-quality tests are complete.

## Water classes

1. human potable
2. food-processing clean water
3. animal drinking
4. irrigation
5. service/wash water
6. fire water

Do not over-treat every stream to potable quality; match treatment to use.

## Required inputs

- source flow
- bacteria
- arsenic if groundwater
- iron/manganese where relevant
- pH
- EC/salinity
- turbidity
- hardness/other site-specific contaminants
- daily and peak demand by water class

## Possible process train

source → raw-water storage/settling → media filtration → contaminant-specific treatment → disinfection where required → treated storage → pressure/distribution zones.

Rainwater may require first-flush and filtration before appropriate reuse.

## Placement and safety

- above flood contamination risk
- wet equipment physically separated from live electrical rooms
- chemical dosing locked/contained
- sample taps before/after treatment
- bypass/isolation for maintenance
- separate potable tank/distribution where needed

## Blueprint output

Show source, tanks, treatment units, pumps, sampling points, valves, backflow prevention, potable/non-potable networks, drainage and emergency bypass.
