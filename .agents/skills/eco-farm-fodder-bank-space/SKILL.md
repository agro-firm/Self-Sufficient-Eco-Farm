# Fodder Bank Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**12,000 m²** — largest internal productive block.

Typical split:
- Napier ~5,500–6,000 m²
- fodder maize/sorghum ~2,500–3,000 m²
- legume forage ~1,500–2,000 m²
- seasonal fodder + controlled azolla/duckweed + access/headlands = balance

## Required design

- staggered harvest blocks
- machine/cart access
- drainage to avoid waterlogging
- irrigation lines
- silage-haul route
- seed/planting-material section
- emergency buffer production

## Capacity rule

Animal numbers may increase only after measured annual dry-matter production + stored reserve demonstrate capacity with safety margin.

## Image rules

The fodder bank must visually remain the largest productive internal block. Do not shrink it to make space for decorative or nonessential facilities.
