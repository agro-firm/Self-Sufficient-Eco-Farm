# Garage and Workshop Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and the processing/storage hub authority. Exact dimensions remain `PROVISIONAL` until the approved CAD layout.

## Functions

- car parking
- tractor parking
- implement parking
- maintenance bay
- tool room
- spare-parts store
- battery/electric-tool charging
- lubricant and service-fluid storage
- controlled welding/hot-work area
- tire/parts handling
- wash point if approved

## Placement rules

- service-side access only
- direct route to service gate and fields
- no heavy vehicle route through house/playground
- separated from clean packhouse and hay/fire-load storage
- oily drainage never enters soil, crop drains, or canal
- rooftop solar may be used if structural design permits

## Safety

- oil/chemical secondary containment
- oil-water separator for contaminated wash/runoff
- fire extinguishers and hot-work controls
- ventilation
- no uncontrolled fuel storage
- lockable chemicals/tools
- spill kit
- clear tractor turning and implement clearance

## Image/blueprint output

Show vehicle bays, tractor/implement footprint, maintenance clearances, doors, turning path, spare room, hot-work zone, oil/chemical zone, drains, oil-water separator route, electrical points and fire equipment.
