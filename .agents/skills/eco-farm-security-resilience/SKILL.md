# Security and Disaster Resilience

## Workflow
1. Protect perimeter with wall, inspection strip, canal, gates, lighting, and CCTV.
2. Maintain two independent vehicle access points.
3. Ensure fire/service road reaches all critical zones.
4. Keep energy, seed, medicine, controls, and critical storage above flood level.
5. Use engineered spillways, check valves, erosion protection, and backflow control.
6. Use BNBC-based wind design, roof anchoring, PV uplift checks, and tree setbacks.
7. Maintain emergency food, feed, water, medical, communications, pump, and electrical spares.
8. Keep playground/family routes away from canal and service traffic.

## Spatial design requirement

Show wall, gates, canal crossings, fire road, CCTV coverage sectors, lighting points, emergency assembly point, critical flood-elevation zones, fire-water suction, and emergency access.

## Image/blueprint requirement

No blind gate approach, no single-point emergency access, and no playground placement beside canal/heavy service traffic.
