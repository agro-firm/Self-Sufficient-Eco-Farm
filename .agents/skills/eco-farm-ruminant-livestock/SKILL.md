# Ruminant Livestock

## Base concept
Cattle 6–8, goats 12–16, sheep 6–8 until audits justify expansion.

## Workflow
1. Confirm feed, water, heat stress, labor, manure handling, and veterinary support.
2. Separate resting, feeding, maternity, youngstock, exercise, isolation, and manure lanes.
3. Keep drinking water separate from wash/manure systems.
4. Track milk, births, mortality, body condition, feed, disease, and medicine withdrawal.
5. Route manure to biogas/compost on the shortest dirty-service path.
6. Review expansion against feed, energy, water, and treatment headroom.

## Guardrails
Veterinary diagnosis, vaccination, medicines, and breeding interventions require appropriate professional guidance.
