# Economics and Operations

## Workflow
1. Separate CAPEX, OPEX, replacement reserves, and emergency reserves.
2. Value sold and internally consumed outputs: feed, energy, fertilizer, seed, water.
3. Include mortality, spoilage, maintenance, labor, testing, veterinary, compliance, and replacement cycles.
4. Model ramp-up years separately from mature operation.
5. Stress-test flood/cyclone, crop failure, feed prices, disease, equipment failure, and market prices.
6. Report cash flow, break-even, payback ranges, self-sufficiency ratios, and working-capital needs.

## Guardrails
Use conservative/base/upside scenarios, not one optimistic case.
