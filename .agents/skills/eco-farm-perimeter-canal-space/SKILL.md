# Perimeter Canal Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Fixed decision

There is **no separate base-plan fish pond**.

## Planning concept

- planning allocation: 5,400 m²
- clear-water width: ~6.0 m concept
- operating depth: ~1.5–2.0 m concept
- continuous loop except engineered gate/bridge crossings

## Required zones within the canal system

- nursery/hapa management section
- grow-out management sections
- screened irrigation intake
- emergency fire-water suction points
- overflow/spillway section
- fish-escape screens
- maintenance/aeration points
- monitoring points

## Hydrology rules

- flood storage comes before maximum fish density
- no sewage, raw manure, oily water, or untreated processing water
- clean field runoff enters only through sediment/erosion control
- bridge/culvert geometry must not create destructive flow bottlenecks
- bank erosion must be controlled

## Blueprint rules

Show inner and outer bank lines, waterline, width, crossings, screens/sluices, intake, overflow, fire points, and maintenance access. Never draw a central pond as a substitute.

## Engineering stop conditions

Final bank slope, lining, excavation, freeboard, overflow level, culverts, and stocking density require survey/hydraulic/geotechnical/aquaculture evidence.
