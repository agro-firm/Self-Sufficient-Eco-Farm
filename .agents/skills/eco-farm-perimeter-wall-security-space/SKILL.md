# Perimeter Wall and Security Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning envelope

Authority area: wall + inspection/privacy system = **1,800 m² planning allocation**.

Concept sequence:
outside vegetation → wall → inspection strip → canal.

Concept values:
- wall height ~3.0 m
- 1,800 m² gross allocation = equivalent ~1.93 m average perimeter band including wall/inspection/privacy functions
- clear inspection/service width may widen locally to ~2.5–3.0 m at gates, CCTV and maintenance bays; it is not a continuous 3 m strip
- final wall thickness/footing not fixed

## Required components

- legal boundary control markers
- reinforced wall/gate piers as engineered
- inspection/patrol strip
- low/medium privacy vegetation within the perimeter allocation
- tall privacy/windbreak trees only on approved non-shading edges using orchard/buffer allocation, or on additional land outside the 5.5 ha authority
- CCTV sight lines
- security lighting
- gatehouse interfaces
- maintenance access
- anti-climb/top detail only if locally appropriate and approved
- drainage so wall footing is never ponded

## Adjacency rules

Must touch: legal boundary, gate systems, inspection strip.
Must not be undermined by: canal excavation, tree roots, uncontrolled drain outlets.

## Image/blueprint rules

Show the wall as the outer security edge. Never place the canal outside the wall unless an approved civil decision changes the section. Blueprint must show wall line, gate openings, inspection strip width, canal bank setback, and CCTV/light positions.

## Engineering stop conditions

Do not fix footing, reinforcement, wall thickness, expansion joints, foundation depth, or canal-wall setback without geotechnical and structural design.
