# Land, Civil and Zoning

## Workflow
1. Preserve 250 m × 220 m planning basis unless an approved survey replaces it.
2. Reserve wall, inspection strip, canal, fire/service road, and biosecurity buffer first.
3. Maintain main clean gate and service/emergency gate.
4. Reserve 5 m primary roads, 3.5 m secondary roads, and 4.5–5 m fire/service routes as concepts.
5. Check turning, loading, fire access, pedestrian safety, playground separation, and mature-tree shadow.
6. Mark exact earthwork/foundation quantities as pending survey/geotechnical design.

## Stop conditions
Do not finalize wall footing, canal cross-section, embankment height, building pad level, or foundations without topo/flood/geotechnical evidence.
