# Ruminant Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**3,200 m²**.

Base herd:
- cattle 6–8
- goats 12–16
- sheep 6–8

Concept internal allocations:
- cattle shed ~350–450 m²
- cattle yard ~500–700 m²
- goat/sheep area ~300–400 m²
- maternity/calf/isolation ~250–350 m²
- feed/manure lanes and buffers = balance

## Required functions

resting, feeding, milking/hygiene, maternity, youngstock, exercise, quarantine interface, clean water, shade/ventilation, manure collection.

## Flow rules

Feed enters clean side.
Animals move within controlled lanes.
Manure exits dirty side directly toward biogas/compost.
No manure vehicle crosses packhouse/playground routes.

## Image rules

Show realistic working livestock sheds and paddocks, not open mixed animals wandering across the whole farm.
