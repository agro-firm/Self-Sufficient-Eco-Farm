# Eco-Farm Quality Gate

Before accepting significant work, check:

1. Authority and 5.5 ha decisions
2. Land totals and physical plausibility, including perimeter-ring closure and parent-zone subspace sums
3. Crop sunlight
4. Clean/dirty water separation
5. Flood retention/overflow/backflow/elevation
6. Biosecurity and traffic crossings
7. Feed dry matter and reserves
8. Waste/manure treatment capacity
9. Critical energy protection and evidence-based sizing, including biogas generator vs measured gas production
10. Processing hygiene/dust/wet/fire separation
11. Emergency gates/roads/power/water/feed/comms
12. Concept vs measured/engineered evidence
13. Compliance/professional approvals
14. Operational ownership/maintenance/KPIs

## Outcome
Return one:
- Ready for next design phase
- Ready with listed assumptions
- Blocked pending specific evidence/engineering

Never approve a concept as construction-ready merely because the diagram looks complete.

## Precision/visualization gate

For every blueprint/image also verify:
- north orientation is consistent
- plot ratio is 250:220
- one image shows one requested angle unless composite requested
- no exact measurement is invented from imagery
- all shown dimensions match approved registry
- fixed adjacency and clean/dirty boundaries remain unchanged
- no extra pond, fountain, resort lawn, or unapproved building was added
