# Vegetable, Greenhouse and Nursery Space

## Precision authority

Use `docs/SPATIAL_DESIGN_STANDARD.md` and `docs/SPACE_REGISTRY.md`.
- Plot basis: 250,000 mm × 220,000 mm.
- Exact centimeter/mm values are allowed only when approved in the coordinate registry or an approved engineering drawing.
- Otherwise mark dimensions/coordinates `PROVISIONAL`.
- Never derive an exact dimension from a photorealistic image.

## Planning allocation

**4,500 m²**.

Typical split:
- open rotation beds ~2,500–3,000 m²
- greenhouse ~700–1,000 m²
- nursery/mother plants ~250–350 m²
- wash/path/drain/service = balance

## Greenhouse concept

Example modules: 10×30 m or 12×24 m; ridge ~4–5 m concept. Final structural/wind design is not fixed.

## Rules

- clean access only
- no animal manure traffic
- no raw slurry irrigation
- disease rotation planning
- dedicated nursery hygiene
- trellis/tall protected crops must not shade neighboring low crops excessively
- drain and irrigation routes shown on plan

## Image rules

Show greenhouse and nursery as compact production structures, not decorative glasshouses.
