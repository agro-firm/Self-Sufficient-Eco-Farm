# Crops and Horticulture

## Workflow
1. Start from soil pH, EC/salinity, fertility, drainage, irrigation quality, and climate.
2. Protect the crop solar envelope from tall trees and structures.
3. Plan rotations across rice, maize, pulses, oilseeds, vegetables, and legumes.
4. Keep greenhouse/nursery hygiene separate from dirty animal traffic.
5. Plan orchard mature-canopy spacing, wind protection, access, and pollinators.
6. Map crop by-products to food, feed, mulch, compost, biogas, or disposal.
7. Provide monthly/quarterly/yearly ranges with assumptions.

## Guardrails
Raw manure wastewater is not crop irrigation. Nutrient application follows soil tests and crop demand.
