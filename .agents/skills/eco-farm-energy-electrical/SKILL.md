# Renewable Energy and Electrical

## Current concept
Solar 120–160 kWp; usable LFP battery 300–500 kWh; biogas generator 25–40 kW dispatchable backup.

## Workflow
1. Build hourly/seasonal load schedules.
2. Split critical and noncritical buses.
3. Prioritize water, cold chain, communications, CCTV, medicine cooling, and aeration.
4. Schedule mills, workshop, and irrigation around solar surplus.
5. Keep critical electrical rooms above flood level.
6. Plan underground corridors, surge protection, earthing, lightning protection, isolation, ATS, and load shedding.
7. Run shade and wind-uplift studies for PV.

## Stop conditions
Final cables, breakers, earthing, PV anchors, battery fire design, and synchronization require licensed engineering.

## Spatial design requirement

Map every electrical room, PV roof, generator, main distribution, critical/noncritical circuits, underground utility corridor, road light, CCTV power/network path, earthing/lightning zones, and isolation point.

## Image/blueprint requirement

A wiring/system image must clearly distinguish power, communications, CCTV, road lighting, generator, battery, solar, and critical-load routes. Do not draw arbitrary cable routes across crop beds or water channels.
