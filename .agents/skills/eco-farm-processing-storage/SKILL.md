# Processing and Storage

## Core systems
Receiving/weighing, drying, grain store, rice mill, feed mill, optional oil press, seed bank, cold room/packhouse, clean milk/egg/fish handling, workshop/spares, silage/hay logistics.

## Workflow
1. Separate dusty, dirty, wet, clean-food, cold, and hot-work zones.
2. Size storage around harvest peaks and internal consumption. Use 10–20 t grain storage as the current base concept; treat 25–40 t as expansion/custom-processing capacity.
3. Keep the total processing/storage subspace schedule within the authoritative 2,800 m² hub, including circulation/fire separation.
4. Preserve bran, broken rice, husk, oil cake, and by-products.
5. Keep clean food paths separate from animal/manure traffic.
6. Put cold chain on the critical electrical bus.
7. Maintain FIFO, batch IDs, moisture, pest control, hygiene, and traceability.
8. Separate hay/fire load from welding/workshop hazards.
