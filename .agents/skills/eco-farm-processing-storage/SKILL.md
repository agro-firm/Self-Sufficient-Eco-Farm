# Processing and Storage

## Core systems
Receiving/weighing, drying, grain store, rice mill, feed mill, optional oil press, seed bank, cold room/packhouse, clean milk/egg/fish handling, workshop/spares, silage/hay logistics.

## Workflow
1. Separate dusty, dirty, wet, clean-food, cold, and hot-work zones.
2. Size storage around harvest peaks.
3. Preserve bran, broken rice, husk, oil cake, and by-products.
4. Keep clean food paths separate from animal/manure traffic.
5. Put cold chain on the critical electrical bus.
6. Maintain FIFO, batch IDs, moisture, pest control, hygiene, and traceability.
7. Separate hay/fire load from welding/workshop hazards.
