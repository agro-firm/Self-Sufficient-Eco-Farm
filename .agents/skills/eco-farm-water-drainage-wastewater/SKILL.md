# Water, Drainage and Wastewater

## Required separation
Clean stormwater; field runoff; human blackwater; greywater; livestock wastewater; poultry/duck wastewater; processing washwater; workshop oily water.

## Workflow
1. Map source → pretreatment → treatment → reuse/outfall for every stream.
2. Keep stormwater out of sewage plants.
3. Use first flush, silt traps, swales, erosion control, and controlled canal inlets.
4. Pretreat solids/grease/oil before biological treatment.
5. Use check valves, spillways, and safe outfalls for flood conditions.
6. Mark pipe sizes/slopes as concepts only.
7. Verify potable/food water separately from irrigation/service water.

## Stop conditions
Exact hydraulics, sewage reuse, potable treatment, and discharge criteria require engineering and approvals.

## Spatial design requirement

Every drain must be mapped from source to final treatment/outfall. The drawing must distinguish clean stormwater, field runoff, human blackwater, greywater, livestock wastewater, poultry/duck wastewater, processing wastewater, and oily workshop water. Exact pipe sizes/inverts/slopes remain provisional until hydraulic design.

## Image/blueprint requirement

Use unique line styles/colors and a legend. Show manholes, silt traps, grease/oil separation, treatment cells, check valves, overflow, and canal inlet points. Never draw a single combined drain network.
