# Spatial Design Standard

## Purpose

This standard controls every future site plan, blueprint, CAD drawing, dimension schedule, 3D model, render, image-generation prompt, and space-specific agent skill for the Self-Sufficient Eco Farm.

## Authoritative coordinate system

- Plot planning basis: **250.000 m × 220.000 m**
- CAD basis: **250,000 mm × 220,000 mm**
- Origin: **south-west corner = (0, 0)**
- +X direction: east
- +Y direction: north
- North must always be shown on plans.
- A plan that rotates the farm for presentation must still preserve the coordinate system in metadata.

## Precision levels

### L0 — Concept
Use meters and planning envelopes. Suitable for ideas, zoning, narratives, and early images.

### L1 — Coordinated master plan
Use coordinates and dimensions to the nearest 0.10 m where verified. All zones must close geometrically and total land must reconcile.

### L2 — Detailed design
Use millimeters for buildings, roads, utilities, drains, openings, equipment pads, and setbacks. Requires approved survey/engineering inputs.

### L3 — Construction issue
Use signed/approved engineering drawings, levels, slopes, sections, details, tolerances, materials, and BOQ references.

## Zero-fabrication rule

A skill may be centimeter- or millimeter-accurate **only to an approved coordinate/dimension authority**. If an exact coordinate has not yet been approved, the skill must label it `PROVISIONAL` and must not invent false precision.

## Dimension hierarchy

1. Survey control and legal boundary
2. Approved master coordinate registry
3. Approved civil/architectural/engineering drawing
4. Space skill envelope
5. Visualization/image

An image never overrides a dimension schedule or CAD authority.

## Required drawing dimensions

Every dimensioned plan must show, as applicable:
- plot overall dimensions
- wall line
- canal clear-water width and bank lines
- inspection strip
- fire/service road
- zone boundaries
- building footprints
- setbacks
- gate widths
- road widths
- drain/swale centerlines and widths
- utility corridors
- tree rows and mature-canopy exclusion zones
- equipment pads
- access/turning areas
- north arrow
- scale
- revision/date
- precision level

## Master planning concepts

Until detailed engineering replaces them:
- security wall: ~3 m high concept
- inspection strip: ~2.5–3.0 m
- perimeter canal clear-water width: ~6 m
- perimeter canal operating depth: ~1.5–2.0 m concept
- inner fire/service road: ~4.5–5.0 m
- primary internal road: ~5.0 m
- secondary internal road: ~3.5 m
- pedestrian path: ~1.2–1.5 m
- main gate: ~6 m clear concept
- service/emergency gate: ~5 m clear concept

These are not construction values until verified.

## Tolerance policy

For visualization:
- relative positions: zero intentional deviation
- zone adjacency: zero intentional deviation
- total plot proportions: preserve 250:220 ratio
- dimensions shown in labels: must match registry exactly
- no decorative object may occupy reserved safety/utility space

For construction:
- tolerances come from the relevant engineer/specification, not from this document.
