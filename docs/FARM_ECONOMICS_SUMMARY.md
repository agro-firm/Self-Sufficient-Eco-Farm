This is **not farm profit**.

It excludes or only partially reflects:
- security, roads, admin and quarantine cost

- biogas/wastewater O&M
- depreciation and financing
- owner/family labor policy
- CAPEX recovery
- market losses, crop failure, animal disease and catastrophic events

## Main business logic

1. **Fodder is an internal-value engine.** It reduces purchased feed, even if no cash sale occurs.
2. **Processing/storage is a loss-reduction and value-retention hub.** It should not be oversized unless custom processing demand exists.
3. **Energy/water and biogas are cost-displacement systems.** Their value is avoided purchased electricity/fuel/fertilizer/water treatment.
4. **Security, roads, quarantine and buffers are protection systems.** Their economic benefit is avoided loss and downtime.
5. **Vegetables, fruit, milk, eggs and fish create recurring cash flow**, but their prices and disease/spoilage risks are more volatile.
6. **Food crops support resilience first**, with surplus sale as secondary value.

## Farm-level accounting structure

Maintain separate ledgers for:
- direct sales
- internal transfers
- household consumption
- purchased inputs
- hired labor
- utilities/fuel
- veterinary/health
- packaging/transport
- maintenance/spares
- replacement reserve
- losses/mortality/spoilage
- CAPEX/depreciation
- finance/tax/fees if applicable
