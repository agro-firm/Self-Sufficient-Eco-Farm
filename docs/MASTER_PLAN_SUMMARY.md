# Self-Sufficient Eco Farm — Master Plan Summary

## Authoritative planning basis

- Land: **5.5 hectares = 55,000 m² ≈ 13.59 acres**
- Planning geometry: **250 m × 220 m**
- Climate basis: Bangladesh tropical monsoon conditions
- Goal: low-external-dependency, near-zero operational-carbon, flood/cyclone-resilient integrated farm

## Key systems

### Perimeter
Concept wall height ~3 m. The 1,800 m² security/inspection allocation is an equivalent ~1.93 m average perimeter band, with wider local service bays. Continuous canal concept ~6 m clear-water width and ~1.5–2.0 m operating depth, subject to engineering.

### Roads
The 4,200 m² road allocation is reserved mainly for the continuous perimeter fire/service ring (~4.9 m average) plus gates/edge drains. Internal ~5 m primary and ~3.5 m secondary access spines are counted inside the separate 3,000 m² internal-access/buffer allocation. Main clean gate and separate service/emergency gate remain required.

### Animals
Self-sufficiency base: cattle 6–8; goats 12–16; sheep 6–8; layers 120–150; broilers 80–120/batch; ducks 60–80. Expansion only after feed, water, energy, labor, health, and waste-treatment audits.

### Processing
Grain receiving/drying, 10–20 t base grain storage (25–40 t only for expansion/custom processing), compact rice mill ~100–200 kg paddy/hour base, feed mill, optional oil press, seed bank, cold room/packhouse, clean milk/egg/fish handling, workshop/spares, and silage/hay logistics.

### Energy
Concept: rooftop solar 120–160 kWp, usable LFP battery 300–500 kWh, biogas generator sized to measured gas production; 10–20 kW initial planning envelope, with 25–40 kW only if future feedstock mass balance supports it. Final sizing requires hourly load and manure/gas mass-balance studies.

### Water and drainage
No untreated human, animal, processing, or oily workshop waste may enter the fish canal. Stormwater is handled separately.

### Safety
Flood elevation hierarchy, BNBC-based cyclone/wind design, two-gate emergency access, fire/service road, canal fire-water points, CCTV, lighting, quarantine, and emergency reserves.

## Design status

All dimensions and capacities above are master-planning concepts, not final construction values. Site survey, geotechnical work, soil/water tests, flood-level study, structural design, hydraulic calculations, electrical protection design, veterinary planning, environmental compliance, and fire engineering are required before construction.
