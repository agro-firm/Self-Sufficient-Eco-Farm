# Farm Decision Log

## D-001 — Authoritative land size
**Status:** Accepted  
Use 5.5 ha = 55,000 m² with a 250 m × 220 m planning rectangle.

## D-002 — Perimeter canal replaces separate fish pond
**Status:** Accepted  
Use the perimeter canal as fish system, flood buffer, irrigation reserve, fire-water reserve, and security separation.

## D-003 — Self-sufficiency-first livestock sizing
**Status:** Accepted  
Start conservatively. Expansion follows measured feed, waste-treatment, energy, water, labor, and health KPIs.

## D-004 — Processing and storage are core infrastructure
**Status:** Accepted  
Include grain storage, rice milling, feed mixing, cold chain, seed storage, workshop/spares, and feed reserves.

## D-005 — Wastewater streams remain separated
**Status:** Accepted  
Separate clean stormwater, human blackwater/greywater, livestock wastewater, poultry/duck wastewater, processing washwater, and workshop oily water.

## D-006 — Energy strategy
**Status:** Accepted  
Prefer rooftop solar, battery storage, and biogas backup.

## D-007 — Clean/dirty route separation
**Status:** Accepted  
Main gate serves clean/family/food flows. Service gate serves livestock, manure, workshop, heavy machinery, and industrial logistics.

## D-008 — Playground
**Status:** Accepted  
Reserve approximately 20 m × 30 m (600 m²) near the house/admin zone, away from canal edges, workshop traffic, biogas, quarantine, and chemical storage.

## D-009 — Perimeter geometry reconciliation
**Status:** Accepted after full-space audit  
The 1,800 m² wall/inspection/privacy allocation is an equivalent ~1.93 m average perimeter band, not a continuous 2.5–3.0 m clear strip. Local bays may widen. The 5,400 m² canal remains ~6 m wide at concept level. The 4,200 m² road allocation is used primarily by the continuous ~4.9 m perimeter fire/service road ring. Internal access spines are counted inside the 3,000 m² internal-access/buffer allocation.

## D-010 — Processing hub capacity reconciliation
**Status:** Accepted after full-space audit  
All processing/storage subspaces must sum to no more than 2,800 m² including circulation/fire separation. Base grain storage is 10–20 t; 25–40 t is an expansion/custom-processing option. Base rice mill capacity is ~100–200 kg paddy/hour; larger units require a throughput/business justification.

## D-011 — Biogas generator must follow gas mass balance
**Status:** Accepted after full-space audit  
Do not treat 25–40 kW as guaranteed base capacity. Use 10–20 kW as an initial planning envelope for the conservative herd, and procure/size only after measured manure/organic feedstock, gas yield, storage, and runtime analysis. A 25–40 kW unit is a future option only if the mass balance supports it.
